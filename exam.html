02Format形式

Format 形式

  • 2 hours, 100 marks, all questions compulsory, answers written on the paper.
  • Calculator allowed (approved models). You will multiply big numbers: params and ops counts.
  • Your section (Chan & Chin, subclasses A/B) asks 6–8 questions: short explanations, fill-in tables, and 2–3 calculations.
  • Subclass C/D papers (a different lecturer) ask 3–4 long "construct the weights" puzzles. Useful practice, but your paper will look like the A/B ones.

Where the marks went 分數分佈

  • CNN arithmetic appears in every A/B paper: output size, parameter count, multiplication count, receptive field.
  • Attention by hand (dot product → softmax → weighted sum) appears in 3 of 4 recent papers.
  • GAN appeared in 3 of 4 A/B papers, each worth 12–20 marks.
  • 2025 papers added LLM topics: LoRA, quantisation, hallucination, prompting.
